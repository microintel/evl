## What is EVL?

EVL is a simple app that helps you build your English vocabulary. The words are split into **180 small lists of 20 words**, so you always know what to study next and never feel overwhelmed.

It remembers what you know and what you find tricky, and brings words back at the right time so they actually stick.

## What you can do

- **Study list by list** – Pick a list and learn its 20 words at your own pace.
- **See what each word means** – Tap any word to read its definitions and example sentences.
- **Hear how it sounds** – Tap the speaker icon to listen to the pronunciation.
- **Practice in different ways**
  - **Flashcards** – flip through words and check yourself
  - **Quiz** – choose the right meaning
  - **Spelling** – type the word you hear or see
  - **Mixed** – a bit of everything
- **Review at the right time** – Words you know well come back less often; words you miss come back sooner. Open **Review** to see what's due today.
- **Set a daily goal** – Choose 10, 20, 30, 50 or 100 words a day and watch your progress ring fill up.
- **Build a streak** – Study every day to keep your streak going.
- **Track your progress** – See how many words you have mastered, your accuracy, a 14-day activity chart, and the words you find trickiest.
- **Pick your look** – Switch between a White and a Black theme.

## Use it on your phone

EVL is made to feel like a real mobile app.

1. Open the EVL website in your phone's browser.
2. **iPhone:** tap *Share* → *Add to Home Screen*.
   **Android:** tap the menu (⋮) → *Install app* or *Add to Home screen*.
3. Open it from your home screen like any other app.

On a computer, EVL shows a sidebar for easy navigation. On a phone, it shows tabs at the bottom.

## Works offline

After you have opened EVL once, it keeps working without internet. Word lists and your progress are always available. Definitions you have already looked up are saved too, so you can read them again offline.

## Your progress is private

- Everything is saved **on your own device**. There are no accounts and no sign-up.
- Your progress is not sent to any server.
- Word definitions are fetched from the free [dictionaryapi.dev](https://dictionaryapi.dev) service.

> **Good to know:** Because progress lives on your device, clearing your browser data will erase it. Use the backup feature below to keep it safe.

## Back up your progress

Go to **Progress → Backup**:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Export** | Saves your progress as a file you can keep |
| **Import** | Restores progress from a saved file (handy on a new phone) |
| **Reset all** | Erases all progress and starts fresh |

## Tips for learning faster

- Study a little **every day** instead of a lot once in a while.
- Do your **Review** first, then move on to a new list.
- Say the word out loud after you hear it.
- Try the **Spelling** mode for words you keep forgetting.

## Common questions

**Is it free?**
Yes.

**Do I need to create an account?**
No. Just open it and start learning.

**The definition says "Offline". What do I do?**
That word's definition hasn't been downloaded yet. Connect to the internet and tap **Retry**.

**I can't hear any sound.**
Check that **Audio** is turned on in **Progress → Settings**, and that your device volume is up.

**My progress disappeared.**
This can happen if browser data was cleared. Restore it with **Import** if you made a backup.
